Lancer:

King is weak to physical attacks! I recommend using Kris and Susie as your main damage dealers. Build up TP quickly and unleash combined spells when you get the chance. Defense boosts are crucial in this fight - don't neglect healing!

Ralsei:

Make sure to equip the best armor and weapons you've found before the battle. Use Ralsei's healing magic to keep your party healthy. Balance offense and defense - rush down King quickly but don't sacrifice survivability. Item management is key!

Noelle:

Use magic attacks! Freeze spell can be very effective. I recommend having Noelle in your party for support. The fight is about pattern recognition - once you learn King's attack patterns, it becomes much easier. Stay alert!
